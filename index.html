    What Boldemort does

    Boldemort helps you add emphasis where formatting is limited, such as LinkedIn posts and plain-text email. Write in Markdown or copy styled Unicode text.

    • Switch between Markdown and Unicode in one draft.
    • Apply bold, italic, underline, or strikethrough and copy the result.
    • Check writing stats and LinkedIn length before you post, or export your draft as a file.
